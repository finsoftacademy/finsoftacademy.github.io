OUR STORY

The Story Behind FinSoft Academy

FinSoft Academy was founded by Diodorus Makatu while he was pursuing a Bachelor of Accounting with Information Technology at the Institute of Finance Management (IFM).

The idea started from a simple question: How can accounting students become confident not only with accounting concepts, but also with the technology and systems used to apply them?

To develop the idea, Diodorus attended several accounting trainings, information technology trainings, professional and social skills sessions, while also gaining practical exposure through work with an accounting firm and a software company.

After developing the concept, he found a team of like-minded people who shared the vision. FinSoft Academy was then established to help students and other learners bridge the gap between academic accounting knowledge and practical application through technology.

Since then, the Academy has supported 2,000+ university and non-university learners through learning opportunities in Accounting, Taxation, Auditing and Information Systems.
